Help Center

Everything you need to know about your autonomous ride.

How does the "Summon" feature work?

Using the NeoViator app, simply tap "Summon". The bike uses GPS and Vision AI to navigate from its parking spot directly to your current location. It obeys traffic laws and avoids obstacles automatically.

Is the autonomous mode safe in heavy traffic?

Yes. The Guardian AI system processes data from 360° sensors 100 times per second. It detects cars, pedestrians, and cyclists faster than a human can, automatically braking or adjusting course to prevent accidents.

What happens if the battery runs out?

The bike monitors its own charge level. If it gets critically low while parked, it can autonomously navigate to the nearest charging dock (in supported cities). You can also remove the battery and charge it at home.

What prevents the bike from being stolen?

NeoViator locks its wheels when not in use. If unauthorized movement is detected, the GPS tracker activates, the alarm sounds, and the bike sends a live location alert to your phone and local authorities.

Do I need a special license to ride?

In most regions, NeoViator is classified as a Class 2 or Class 3 e-bike, meaning no special license is required. However, we recommend checking your local regulations regarding autonomous vehicle usage.
