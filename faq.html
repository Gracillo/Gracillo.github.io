Client Service

Expert assistance for your L'AURA pieces.

How do I determine my size?

Our sizing is based on Italian tailoring standards (EU 46-56). Each product page features a detailed "Size Guide" with specific measurements for chest, waist, and sleeve length. We recommend visiting a local tailor for your exact measurements before ordering.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, L'AURA ships globally. We offer complimentary express delivery (1-3 business days) for all orders over €500. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout.

What is the return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, with all original tags and security seals intact. Custom-tailored or "Made to Measure" pieces are final sale.

How do I care for my leather goods?

L'AURA leathers are natural and untreated. Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight or rain. We recommend professional leather cleaning only.
